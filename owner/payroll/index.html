Running payroll Wednesday

  1. In Square Payroll, choose Import time and wages so hours come in from timecards.
  2. Type each person’s Tips amount from this page into Paycheck Tips.
  3. Check the totals, then submit.

Keep tip importing turned off in Square Payroll settings, so Square doesn’t add its own tip split on top.

Tips by day

Each day, baristas get the share of card tips that matches how much of open hours a barista was on. That share is split by the time each barista worked. Tips from hours with no barista on are set aside.
